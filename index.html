webmaster@qwertywelch.com ↗

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and level number. A screenshot helps. Never send your Apple password or payment-card details.

Pick up your puzzle

Pause a puzzle and choose Save & home. Tap Resume on the home screen to continue on the same device. Journey and daily puzzles are saved separately.

Overall progress can sync through iCloud. In-progress boards stay on the device where you started them.

Room for another round

The first ten levels use no lives. Later wins return the life used to enter. You can hold eight lives; one returns every ten minutes.

The Daily Puzzle is free to play without lives. Replays stay available after its daily reward is collected.

Purchases and Lantern Club

For a missing purchase, open Little extras → Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account. A purchase awaiting approval arrives after Apple approves it.

Manage or cancel Lantern Club through Munchlight Settings → Manage subscription, or your Apple Account subscription settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Something went wrong?

Email the support address above with what happened and the steps that led to it. If the app reports a save problem, keep it open and try saving again before reinstalling.
